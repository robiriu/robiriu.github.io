Ongoing: Enterprise AI Automation¶

Agentic ERP Automation Platform LIVE IN PRODUCTION¶

An agentic automation platform layered on a live commercial cloud ERP, running the whole supply chain of a multi-entity food manufacturing group: buy, receive, make, sell to the group's own outlets, and pay. Purchase orders, partial goods receipts, AI vision extraction of vendor invoices matched to their receipts, production and work orders from the real bill of materials, an inter-company sales flow from outlet spreadsheet to settlement, stock opname and transfers from a Flutter Android app, and a three-gate payment flow with enforced separation of duties that executes real bank transfers and writes payments back into the ERP.

Built with no ERP sandbox and a hard daily API quota, so every safety property had to be engineered: a GET-only mirror for reads, a seven-layer write-guard with idempotency and read-back verification, timeouts treated as unknown outcomes rather than failures, a shared quota guard across 20 services, and per-capability feature flags canaried one document at a time.

Technologies: Python, FastAPI, Next.js, TypeScript, Flutter, PostgreSQL, Claude (headless CLI, vision, MCP), Docker Compose, Prometheus/Grafana, GitHub Actions

Highlights: 5 operational pillars, 12 guarded ERP write capabilities, ~310 API endpoints, 61 read-only AI tools, 620 automated tests, 75 tables, 1,485 commits, live and moving real money

AI Drawing Platform for Architecture and Interior Design IN USE¶

An internal platform that turns a designer's chat into exact working drawings: floor, finish, ceiling and lighting, electrical and plumbing plans, sections and elevations, a door and window schedule and a quantity take-off, plus a 3D model and a browser walkthrough of the same space. The model never draws: Claude edits a validated JSON spec and deterministic engines render every sheet to exact scale, so every output agrees with every other.

Proved on a real outlet's 15-page drawing set, transcribed and checked by its own clash checker. Sections are cut from the stacked 3D model and re-cut when a floor changes; electrical, plumbing, finish and ceiling sheets are live views of their floor; door and window codes stay stable across revisions.

Technologies: Python, ezdxf, trimesh, FastAPI, three.js, Claude (headless CLI, vision), Blender Cycles, Docker Compose

Highlights: one spec drives every sheet, the quantities and the 3D walkthrough; ~9 s chat edits; PDF import in ~16 s; clash checking fed back to the model

Ongoing: AI Game Studio¶

AI Game Studio Automation for Mimio Entertainment ONGOING¶

An AI automation system that runs a small Roblox game studio from research to publishing. Twelve role agents on headless Claude (research, compliance, design, art direction, Roblox and Unity builders, QA, store, marketing, content, analytics) work through an 18-step launch pipeline; the developer approves or sends work back with notes, and builds the game in Roblox Studio through the Studio MCP. Roblox Open Cloud handles game passes, data checks and live-server tests; a web dashboard and an Obsidian vault show every step.

The first game, Wisteria Hollow, ships with saved progress and a cross-server session lock, Bahasa Indonesia and English, touch controls and four fair game passes, and was renamed and reworked after an AI review flagged names too close to an existing anime.

Technologies: Claude (headless CLI, Opus), Roblox Studio MCP, Luau, Roblox Open Cloud, Python, FastAPI, Docker Compose, YouTube Data API, Obsidian, ffmpeg

Highlights: 12 agents, 18-step pipeline with approval gates, game built and published on Roblox, store art and a YouTube Short produced by the pipeline, engine-agnostic for Unity

Mobile & Kiosk Apps¶

Face-Attendance Android App + Outlet Kiosk FLUTTER LIVE¶

One Flutter codebase shipping as two products: an employee phone app where clocking in is a face verification inside a geofence, and the same binary running as a shared outlet kiosk tablet with no login and no session. The company's attendance clock since October 2026, used every day on outlet tablets.

Built for store floors rather than for a demo: a server-issued liveness challenge with server-tuned coaching limits and a three-frame burst verified server-side, no face embedding ever computed on the device, an offline clock queue replayed with its original timestamp, and NIK + PIN sign-in with no per-login message cost. The kiosk is locked down by a native Kotlin layer (launcher alias, lock task, device owner) that replaced a paid kiosk app, installs its own updates silently, and reopens itself after an update or a power cut, including the Android 15 overlay workaround.

Technologies: Flutter, Dart, Kotlin, Riverpod, go_router, dio, Google ML Kit face detection, camera, geolocator, flutter_secure_storage, video_player, wakelock_plus

Highlights: in daily production use, ~7,600 lines of Dart, over 35 over-the-air releases in the first month, tablet clock cut from about a minute to seconds, self-recovering kiosk tablets, roster-scoped matching with a NIK fallback

Ongoing: Workforce Platforms¶

HR & Face-Attendance Platform LIVE¶

A company-owned HR platform for a multi-site food and beverage business, built on three surfaces over one backend: an Android app where employees clock in by face inside a geofence, the same app running as a locked-down kiosk tablet in the outlets, and an HR web dashboard for attendance, contracts, incidents, salary, recruitment with psychological tests, reimbursement and cash advances, and payroll. The company's attendance system since October 2026.

Face verification runs server-side on CPU (InsightFace ArcFace) with a review band instead of a guess between the accept and reject thresholds, a kiosk that matches only against its own sites' roster for the day, and adaptive templates learned only from unambiguous matches. An attendance rules engine handles night shifts across midnight, breaks, overtime and staff who move between branches. Contract recommendations come from a rule engine with the evidence shown; the model writes the narrative and never produces a number. Biometric handling is designed against Indonesia's UU PDP No. 27/2022.

Technologies: FastAPI, PostgreSQL + PostGIS, Next.js, Flutter, InsightFace + onnxruntime, Claude (headless CLI), MCP, Docker Compose, Prometheus, GitHub Actions

Highlights: dozens of employees clocking by face daily, first-try match 72% to 90% after adaptive templates, ~320 API endpoints, 71 tables, 22 read-only AI tools, 225 backend tests, several hundred employees and dozens of sites on the platform, payroll pending a parallel run

Ongoing: AI-Native Applications¶

Two applications built on the same architectural idea: one shared live state edited by both a human and an AI agent, an MCP tool layer instead of code generation, and a headless Claude Code agent running on a subscription.

TransForce - AI-Native Particle Transport DEPLOYED¶

Chat-driven Monte Carlo radiation transport on OpenMC. Describe a reactor physics problem in plain language and an agent builds the CSG geometry, materials, source and tallies, runs the transport calculation, and reports k-eff with uncertainty. Ingests a paper from an arXiv id, DOI, URL or PDF, extracts a structured model spec, reproduces it, and reports the bias in pcm against the published values.

The agent never writes Python: it manipulates a typed, provider-agnostic ModelSpec, and a deterministic driver translates that into live OpenMC objects. Includes depletion, weight-window variance reduction, MGXS generation, and PDF reporting.

Technologies: Python, FastAPI, FastMCP, OpenMC, Pydantic, React, Vite, matplotlib, reportlab, Docker

Highlights: 27 simulation MCP tools, 4 benchmark presets including Godiva, 3 compute backends (local/VPS/GCP), C/E comparison in pcm, runs on a Claude subscription with no API key

CutForce - AI-Native Video Editor DEPLOYED¶

A browser video editor where a human and an AI agent edit the same timeline simultaneously, over 43 MCP tools and a live WebSocket-synced store, with an embedded terminal wired to the editor's own MCP endpoint. Undo works across both editors because every mutation funnels through one commit path.

The export engine compiles the timeline into a single ffmpeg filter_complex: keyframes become piecewise-linear ffmpeg expressions, speed changes chain atempo stages, and colour grades resolve from the same numbers in both the CSS preview and the ffmpeg render.

Technologies: TypeScript, Express, MCP SDK, React, Vite, zustand, xterm.js, ffmpeg, node-pty, Veo/Imagen/FLUX, Docker

Highlights: 43 MCP tools, 7 authored agent skills, 12 grade presets, cross-editor undo, generative clips with cost estimation, runs anywhere Node and ffmpeg run

Computer Vision & Video Analytics¶

Retail Video Analytics - Customer Behaviour from CCTV COMPLETED¶

An end-to-end pipeline that turns two retail CCTV cameras into behavioural metrics: store interest and walk-in conversion, per-shelf engagement events, and genuine staff-to-customer interaction sessions. YOLO11m detection with ByteTrack identity persistence, entirely on CPU, reproducible from one Docker command.

The engineering is in the layer above the detector. Perception is cached so that behavioural thresholds can be tuned interactively instead of at 80 CPU-minutes per iteration. Speeds and distances are normalised by body height so a single threshold holds across the depth of the frame. Shelf assignment uses the torso rather than the feet, after an audit showed browsing customers' feet stay in the aisle while their upper body leans into the fixture. An apron-colour staff classifier was built, measured, and rejected in favour of a spatial rule that actually separates the classes.

Technologies: Python, YOLO11m (Ultralytics), ByteTrack, PyTorch CPU, OpenCV, pandas, YAML, Docker

Highlights: 2 cameras, 3 behavioural metrics, 10 fps effective inference on CPU, zone geometry validated against plotted real tracks, interpolation guard that stopped a phantom interaction, documented misses
