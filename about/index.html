About Me¶

I am Robi Dany Riupassa, a software engineer and AI specialist with a background in physics research, currently building production-grade AI systems across energy, media, and enterprise domains.

Current Work¶

Developer & Researcher at ForceX AI — Building Indonesia's first AI-for-energy platform with 12 physics-informed AI products spanning nuclear, geothermal, oil & gas, and renewable energy. GeoForce CNN surrogate deployed with R²=0.997, published on HuggingFace.

Software Developer & AI Specialist — Developing enterprise AI systems at a leading ICT solutions provider: agentic RAG chatbots with LangGraph, MLOps pipelines with 24 production models, multimodal AI processing, and cloud-native deployment on Google Cloud Platform.

Background¶

My academic foundation is in physics — I completed doctoral research at Bandung Institute of Technology on passive safety systems for Generation IV Molten Salt Reactors, combining CFD simulation with deep learning for freeze valve design optimization. This background in computational physics directly informs my approach to AI engineering, particularly in physics-informed machine learning and scientific computing.

Teaching¶

I have served as a Training Instructor at the Government Transformation Academy (BPPTIK KOMINFO, Indonesia), delivering Fundamentals of Data Science training for government professionals supporting Indonesia's digital transformation.
