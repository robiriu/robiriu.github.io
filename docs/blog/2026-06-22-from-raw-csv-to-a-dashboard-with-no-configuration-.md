An analytics workspace that turns a raw dataset into decisions - an experiment in zero-configuration dashboards.

## What I was exploring

Most BI tools make you build the dashboard. I wanted to see how much could be inferred automatically from the data alone.

## What it does

Upload a CSV and it builds the dashboard for you: headline KPIs, a trend chart, anomalies flagged across every metric, AI-written insights, and a natural-language layer to ask follow-up questions in plain English.

## What was interesting

Deciding what's worth charting, and flagging anomalies across every metric without being told what "normal" looks like, were the hard and interesting parts.

An MVP - feedback welcome.
